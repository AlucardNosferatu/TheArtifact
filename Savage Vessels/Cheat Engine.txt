Super easy to cheat:
Ammo - just 4 bytes search minus what you have loaded in weapon. I.e. for example, simple chisel gun has 1 charge loaded and you see 40 charges in total, thus you must search for 40 - 1 and viola, you've found it.
Items Amount - same 4 bytes search to look for item amount.
Items IDs - the address you found for Items Amount minus 4 bytes offset.
